The TM Legacy & Philosophy

Generations of Purity & Confectionery Mastery

TM Sweets was founded on an uncompromising principle: honoring timeless traditional recipes with the world's most luxurious ingredients. From pure A2 Desi Ghee and Kashmiri Mamra Almonds to direct-trade Ecuadorian heirloom cacao, each creation is handcrafted with reverence.

100% Pure Desi Cow Ghee & Heritage Saffron

We source authentic clarified butter from grass-fed mountain pastures and hand-picked saffron stigmas from the valleys of Kashmir.

Slow-Conched Fusion Craftsmanship

Our confectionery masters merge traditional brass-pot roasting with Swiss granite conching, achieving unforgettable textural harmony.

Signature Gold Foil Gift Presentation

Every box is wrapped in velvet emerald cases and stamped with the iconic TM Royal Crest, ready for regal gifting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the shelf life of TM Sweets and Chocolates?
Our dry-fruit based royal sweets (Kaju Katli, Pista Rolls) and Dark Chocolates stay pristine for up to 30–45 days at cool room temperature. All orders are packed fresh on the day of dispatch.
Are all TM Sweets prepared with pure Desi Ghee?
Yes! We exclusively use 100% pure certified Cow Ghee with zero palm oils, preservatives, or artificial food coloring.
Do you offer custom wedding & corporate gift hampers?
Yes, we customize luxury gold-embossed boxes with your family crest, wedding monogram, or company logo. Reach out through our contact form.
